A NOTE ABOUT TRUST

Designed to help you decide.

ExamAtlas is a working front-end prototype. Here's what it can and cannot do today.

✅

What works now

Search and filters, pathway browsing, bookmarking, monthly calendar, .ics exports, shareable opportunity links, theme switching and basic personalized demo matching.

🔎

What is sample data

Application dates, exam dates, status badges, match assessments and summaries are illustrative. None should be used to decide when or where to apply.

🛠️

What comes next

Official source collectors, yearly rule verification, correction/approval workflow, reliable deadlines, real eligibility checks and optional notifications.

Always confirm with the organizer.

For any real application, check the latest official notification, eligibility rules and deadline on the organizer's website.
